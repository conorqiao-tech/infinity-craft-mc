# Exploration and scores — 2.12.0-explore.5

Use `/guide` anywhere in the world. It responds to your current place: Main Hub, a mode lobby, a minigame, an adventure map, or a normal game mode. `/hub` returns to the Main Hub, `/lobbies` lists destinations, and `/play` chooses a mode. Tap or right-click the Minigames or Adventure entry sign in the hub, or use `/play minigames` or `/play adventure`, to open a vanilla chest-like course menu. Tap an icon to enter a course. A **SELECT COURSE** sign at each course start reopens the menu, as do the glowing checkpoint lanterns. Direct map commands remain available.

The six minigames are Sky Steps (`/minigame parkour`), Switchback Sprint (`/minigame sprint`), Prism Dropper (`/minigame dropper`), Red Light Run (`/minigame redlight`), Crystal Hunt (`/minigame crystalhunt`), and Color Rush (`/minigame colorrush`). Crystal Hunt asks you to touch five glowing amethyst pads in any order, once each. Color Rush names a pad color in the action bar; reach it within five seconds and repeat for five pulses. Checkpoint, color, and finish hints appear in the action bar. Use `/retry <map>` to replay. `/best` shows your saved personal times; `/leaderboard <map>` shows the saved fastest times across players for any of the six minigames. Records are stored with the world and are rebuilt from player progress if the index is missing. A temporarily unavailable public index does not erase personal times. Your selected map is remembered across reconnects, while an unfinished run restarts.

The two starter adventure maps are The Five Seals (`/adventure ruins`) and Lantern Labyrinth (`/adventure maze`). Creative has a separate building world; entering it gives you an Infinity sword and a named compass that opens its gear picker without a gear command. Survival and Hardcore keep their own inventories and progress. See [MODES.md](MODES.md), [LOBBIES.md](LOBBIES.md), and [EXPANSION.md](EXPANSION.md) for the complete commands and rules. The Creative gear picker and course menus still need a real iPad playtest.

The owner dashboard now presents the modes and maps, live local status, and separate Java and Bedrock join details. Remote addresses appear only when a locally owned Pinggy helper allocates them. They are temporary and need an outside-network test; an allocated address alone does not establish that a friend can join. The public guide site lists content and downloads, but does not run or expose the private dashboard.
