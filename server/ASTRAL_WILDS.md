# Astral Wilds • The Living Circuit

Explore.43 adds a permanent Odyssey chapter: old circuits have guardians again. This is a source candidate until a separately approved installation; these pages do not imply that the live world has changed.

Open **Infinity Menu → Astral Wilds** or **Odyssey Journal → Astral Wilds**. The journal gives exact recipes, six milestone checks, pending Infini credits and your Astral Orbit switch. It opens a touch form on Bedrock and a real chest menu on Java.

## Discover and fight

Guardians are rare **native creature variants**, with native movement, combat and small original particle tells. They are not custom-shaped models or replacements for ordinary creatures.

| Guardian | Where | Behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cinderbound Keeper | Nether and temperate Overworld Odyssey ruins | Fire-resistant husk, 32 health, native melee, ember tell |
| Rimeglass Ranger | Cold Overworld Odyssey ruins | Stray, 24 health, native bow, one brief frost ward |
| Rift Skitter | End Odyssey ruins | Endermite, 16 health, one short collision-checked retreat when wounded |

Walk near an already-loaded natural Odyssey ruin in Survival or Hardcore. One encounter is recorded per ruin. Previously visited loaded ruins can qualify. Peaceful and disabled natural monster spawning are respected. The compass searches loaded terrain; neither menu fabricates a ruin or generates distant chunks.

Chunk unload/reload preserves the guardian's native UUID, health, name and equipment. An unloaded guardian is not replaced. Known terminal removals, including Peaceful despawn and conversion, end their receipt and release the active spawn allowance while keeping that ruin spent. A guardian missing after external entity-file deletion is kept reserved; absence cannot safely be inferred from an unloaded chunk. Resolve such records deliberately as part of a future owner-approved world-recovery operation. The game scans one eligible player every five seconds, with conservative world/nearby caps and bounded encounter history. These budgets protect the server; they do not change player stats.

Verified player kills award the corresponding advancement and a modest native-material bonus. Ordinary mobs receive neither bonus nor Astral progress. Environmental deaths end the encounter without granting player kill credit. Repeated death callbacks cannot deliver the bonus twice.

## Craft the Keeper's tools

Use an ordinary crafting table. `A` = amethyst shard; `E` = echo shard; `G` = glowstone dust; `P` = ender pearl; `S` = string; `H` = iron hoe; `.` = empty.

| Tool | Three recipe rows | How it works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starshard Lantern | `.A.` / `GEG` / `.A.` | Use for 15 seconds of night sight. A visible star bearing points toward a loaded guardian within 64 blocks, when one exists. Ten-second recharge. |
| Riftspool | `.S.` / `PEP` / `.A.` | Use on safe solid ground to anchor; use again within 20 seconds and 64 blocks to return. A successful return costs one plain ender pearl and one durability. Five-second return recharge. |
| Keeper's Trowel | `.A.` / `.H.` / `.E.` | Aim at one growing crop within six blocks. Successful native growth consumes one plain bone meal and one durability. One-second recharge. |

Use the item normally or select **Infinity Menu → Use held power**. Current Admin/OP4 access bypasses ability cooldown refusals; collision, world border, storage, ingredient and session checks still apply. Named ingredients are preserved. Adventure and Spectator cannot use the crop tool to bypass building permissions. Failed, blocked or expired returns consume nothing. Death, reconnecting and world/mode changes cancel a Riftspool anchor.

All three items have original matching Java/Bedrock icons and real Creative catalogue entries. Creative/Admin users can request the kit through the recipe menu with three free inventory slots; no old items are replaced. No rank purchase is required to craft or use them.

## Six advancements and a free constellation

First contact earns 1 Infini. Each of three different guardian kills earns 2. Crafting all three tools earns 3. Finishing all three kills and successfully using all three tools earns 5. Total: **15 Infini**, once per UUID, plus an optional saved **Astral Orbit** cosmetic.

The journal shows actual progress and durable payment status. Failed storage leaves a credit pending; retry safely through the journal or after reconnecting. Repeated callbacks cannot pay twice. Orbit uses a small native star trail and grants no combat advantage. These advancements are server-side progression, separate from platform/Xbox achievement eligibility.
